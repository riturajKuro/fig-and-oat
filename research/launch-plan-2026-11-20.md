## Claim rules (all copy, images and videos)
- ✅ "smooths the look of rough, bumpy skin", "softens dry, rough heels", "visibly smoother-feeling skin", "helps prevent the look of ingrown hairs", "swipe on, no rinse"
- 🚫 treats/cures/eliminates KP, clears breakouts/acne, repairs cracks, fades scars or stretch marks, boosts circulation, "results in 3 days" (INKEY's study, not ours), % of people stats, fake urgency
- 🚫 Competitors' photos (INKEY, Seraphic, Uruses, Bosnow, Wildpier) are layout references only, never used in our store or ads
- AI avatars demonstrate and explain. They never claim to be a customer or describe their own results.
- Show the real product (Lakerain / SUYARUN / white mitt), or keep the packaging out of frame. Never a fake fig&oat-branded tube.

## Phase 3: Product content (Amazon-style)
Per product, a 7-image stack in the brand colours (fig #4A1F3D, oat #F3EADB, honey #B08A5B, leaf #7A8450, Jost):
1. Hero: product on oat background
2. Key ingredients (stick: 7% glycolic, 0.5% salicylic, 10% shea; heel: 60% urea, 2% salicylic; mitt: 100% viscose)
3. How to use: 3 steps
4. Where to use: body map (stick: arms, thighs, bikini line, underarms; heel: heels, elbows, knees, hands; mitt: full body)
5. Why it's different (stick vs creams: no mess, no rinse; mitt: just water + friction)
6. Lifestyle shot (AI-generated scene with the real product inpainted)
7. What's in the box, size, and "ships in manufacturer packaging"

Plus a Routine image set: both products together, "Acids + friction" diagram, weekly routine (stick daily, mitt 1–2× a week).
