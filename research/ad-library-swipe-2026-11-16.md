## Angles ranked for fig&oat
1. **"Acids + friction, not either/or."** The towel brands say acids don't work; the cream brands say scrubbing doesn't work. We sell both, as the stick plus mitt routine. This is the strongest unique angle, and it leads with the Smooth Skin Set or the 2-stick + mitt bundle.
2. **"No-mess swipe-on."** Creams are sticky and need hands. A stick is clean, quick and travel-friendly. Direct contrast with Strawberry's cream, and Equalize proves the format.
3. **One-leg test UGC.** Same person, one treated leg vs one untreated, day 1 / 7 / 14. Needs samples first (to-do #2).
4. **Myth-bust: "It's not your razor."** Counters Freebird's spend: "Switched razors and still bumpy? Those bumps are dead-skin plugs. Swipe, don't scrape."
5. **Gross-out reveal (mitt).** "See what comes off." Use only for mitt or set creatives.
6. **Under-served audiences:** men with bumpy upper arms, and "shorts season" for the US warm states in winter (FL/TX/AZ/CA). Almost no competitor ads target men.

## Compliance guardrails (from what competitors get away with, don't copy)
- Don't say "treats", "cures" or "eliminates" keratosis pilaris. Do say "smooths the look of rough, bumpy skin" and "visibly smoother".
- No "% of adults" stats, no fake clinicians or persona names, no fake countdown timers.
- Compare-at prices must be real before any "% off" claim (see the FTC note in HANDOFF).
- The Heel Stick "urea + salicylic" wording must match the sample's INCI list.

## Offer benchmarks
- Guarantees: Strawberry 60-day "keep the bottle", Equalize 60-day refund, Freebird 30-day. **Our 30-day guarantee is on the short side.** Consider 60 days once margins are known from DSers.
- Bundles: Equalize B2G1, Alorin up to 50% off. Our 3-stick + mitt + heel at $59 is in line.
