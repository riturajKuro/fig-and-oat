## Formula and compliance notes
- Ask for an acids + urea formula (e.g. glycolic/lactic + 10% urea), no retinol in the body serum (irritation risk with acids, more refunds).
- Claims: say "smooths rough, bumpy skin", "visibly smoother in X days". Avoid "treats/cures/corrects KP" or acne claims (drug territory).
- MoCRA: as the brand owner you are the responsible person (facility registration via supplier, product listing, adverse event records).
- Get a COA, full INCI list, MSDS, and ideally stability + patch test reports.
