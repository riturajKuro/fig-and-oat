## Shipping policy

**Processing time**
Orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding holidays). You'll get a confirmation email when your order is placed and a tracking link once it ships.

**Delivery time (United States)**
Most orders arrive within 7–12 business days after they ship. During peak seasons and holidays, delivery can take a few extra days.

**Shipping cost**
Free standard shipping on all US orders.

**Where we ship**
We currently ship to the United States only.

**Tracking**
Every order ships with tracking. If your tracking hasn't updated in 5 business days, email us at concierge@figandoat.com and we'll look into it.

**Multiple packages**
If you order more than one product, your items may arrive in separate packages on different days.

**Lost or damaged parcels**
If your order hasn't arrived within 20 business days of shipping, or arrives damaged, contact us with your order number and a photo (for damage). We'll send a replacement or a full refund.

**Address changes**
Need to change your address? Email us within 12 hours of placing your order. Once an order ships, we can't change the address.

## Refund policy: 30-day smooth-skin guarantee

**The guarantee**
Use it for up to 30 days from delivery. If you don't love how your skin feels, email us and we'll refund your order. You don't need to send the bottle back.

**How to request a refund**
Email concierge@figandoat.com with your order number and a sentence about your experience. We reply within 1–2 business days.

**Refund timing**
Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

**Limits**
- One guarantee refund per customer.
- Bundles are refunded in full under the guarantee.
- Orders over 30 days from delivery aren't eligible.

**Damaged, wrong, or missing items**
Contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo, and we'll send a replacement or a refund. That's separate from the 30-day guarantee.

**Skin reactions**
Stop using the product if irritation occurs, and contact us. We'll refund your order in full.

**Cancellations**
You can cancel within 12 hours of ordering for a full refund. After that, your order may already be processing.
